Data and cookies

Privacy policy

What data coloradocampingspots.com collects, why, who else may receive it and the choices you have.

Revised 10 October 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to coloradocampingspots.com, a Colorado Camping Spots guide owned by the Denver company Eye To Ad Media. If you have questions, reach us through the contact page or at info@eyetoad.com.

Data you choose to give us

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive your name, your email address, the topic and the text of your message, plus the address of the page you were on. The form is handled by a third-party service, FormSubmit, which forwards the submission to our email. We use these details only to read and answer your message and to fix issues you report. We never sell them and do not subscribe you to anything.

Data collected when you browse

Our web host and the third-party services that run on our pages, such as advertising, may receive routine technical data each time you load a page. This includes your IP address, browser and device type, the page you came from and the pages you request. It helps keep the site running, protect it from abuse, measure which guides are read and deliver ads.

Advertising cookies and Google AdSense

We use, or may use, Google AdSense to show advertisements. Here is how that works:

  • Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to show ads that reflect your earlier visits here and elsewhere on the web.
  • Through its advertising cookies, Google and its partners can choose ads for you based on what you have viewed on this website and other websites.
  • You can turn off personalized Google ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • Many other ad companies offer an opt-out at www.aboutads.info.

Opting out does not stop ads from appearing; it makes them less tailored. Google explains how it uses information from websites that use its services on its partner sites page.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent banner powered by a certified consent tool asks for your permission before any personalized ad cookies are used, as the law requires. The banner's settings link lets you change your mind whenever you like. If you do not consent, non-personalized ads may still be shown; these can still use cookies for limited purposes such as limiting how often you see an ad and detecting fraud.

Controlling cookies yourself

Your browser settings let you see, block and delete cookies. Some features of some sites may not work properly if all cookies are blocked.

Other websites

We link frequently to agency and reservation websites such as Recreation.gov, Colorado Parks and Wildlife and the National Park Service. When you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.

Children

This guide is meant for adults planning trips, not for kids under 13, and we never intentionally gather data about children. If a child has sent us personal details, contact us so we can erase them.

Your choices and rights

Privacy laws in some places, including the Colorado Privacy Act where it covers us, let you ask to see, fix or erase information about you, or to limit how it is used. Use the contact page for any such request and we will handle it within the legal timeframe.

How long we keep messages

We hold onto messages for a limited time, just long enough to deal with them and keep basic records, and we protect them sensibly. Online systems are never entirely risk-free.

Updates

When our practices or the law change, we will edit this policy and refresh the revision date shown above.
