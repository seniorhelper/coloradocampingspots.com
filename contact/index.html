Who to contact for other things

  • State park reservations: Colorado Parks and Wildlife at cpwshop.com or 1-800-244-5613.
  • Federal campground and permit reservations: Recreation.gov.
  • Current conditions or fire orders: the ranger district, BLM field office or park that manages the area.
  • Emergencies, wildfire or a lost person: call 911.
