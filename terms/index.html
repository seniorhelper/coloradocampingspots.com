Terms & safety disclaimer

Using this website means you accept these terms. The safety disclaimer is the most important part; please read it.

Revised 10 October 2026

Information, not authority

Colorado Camping Spots publishes general information to help you plan camping trips. Land managers, not this website, decide where and how you may camp. Campground seasons, fees, reservation windows, stay limits, permit requirements and fire restrictions are set by the National Park Service, the U.S. Forest Service, the Bureau of Land Management, Colorado Parks and Wildlife, counties and other agencies, and they change regularly. Our pages may not reflect the latest change.

Before every trip, confirm current rules and conditions with the managing agency, read posted signs and orders at the site, and follow directions from rangers, hosts and emergency officials. If this website and an official source disagree, the official source is correct.

Outdoor activities carry risk

Camping, backpacking and driving in Colorado expose you to hazards such as lightning, high altitude, extreme heat and cold, flash floods, wildfire, falling trees, wildlife, rough roads and remote areas without cell service. Safety information on this site summarizes guidance from public agencies and cannot replace training, good judgment or advice from a doctor or lawyer. Whether a site, route or activity is right for your skills, health and gear is your decision alone. Dial 911 for emergencies.
